# research-coaching

## Inputs and scope

Learner goal, draft/work, prior understanding and preferred coaching depth.

Use the user's requested language and deliverable format. This Skill supplies a workflow; it does not install services or grant access to accounts. Resolve tool availability in the current host before relying on an external backend.

## Workflow

1. Identify the current reasoning gap with a focused question or short reading probe.

2. Use progressively specific prompts and examples; let the researcher articulate a claim and its support.

3. Distinguish teaching feedback from evaluated research findings, and adapt to demonstrated understanding.

4. End with the learner's current decision, remaining misconception and next exercise.

## Deliverable and verification

Coaching dialogue, understanding checks and concrete next steps.

Do not replace the researcher's topic or authorship choices with an unrequested full draft.

Report what was actually inspected, executed and rendered. Preserve existing user work and authorization boundaries for external messages, publishing, paid compute and destructive cleanup.

## Mode details and sources

Read [source contracts](references/source-contracts.md) when selecting an upstream mode, provider adapter or checking feature parity. Those notes distinguish implemented guidance from upstream functionality still requiring tools or verification.

## Example

Help a student turn a broad interest into a testable research question.
